Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Your trust is the whole point of TRYB. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it. In short: we do not sell, trade, or share your data with anyone. We collect it only to provide our services to you.

Our promise on your data. Everything we collect is used solely to deliver TRYB experiences to you — matching you thoughtfully and curating invitations. We never sell it, never trade it, and never share it with third parties for their own marketing.

This Privacy Policy is published by EVELIT ("we", "us", "our"), the registered business that operates the brand TRYB and the website jointryb.in. It is intended to comply with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act").

1.Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run TRYB:

  • Details you give us — your name, email address, phone number, and the answers you provide in our personality and preference questionnaire (interests, availability, location, lifestyle preferences, and similar).
  • Experience information — the invitations you accept, attendance, and the feedback you share after an experience.
  • Technical information — basic, standard information such as device and browser type and how you use the site, used to keep it working and improve it. We use browser storage (localStorage) to save your progress in the form; we do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

2.How we use your information

We use your information for the sole purpose of providing our services to you, including to:

  • understand your personality, interests, and preferences so we can match you well;
  • curate and send you relevant experience invitations;
  • organise and host the experiences you choose to attend;
  • keep our community safe and verify members;
  • respond to your questions and improve TRYB over time.

3.We do not sell or share your data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose your personal data to any third party for their own purposes. Your data stays with us and is used only to provide TRYB services to you.

The only limited exceptions are:

  • Service providers who help us operate (for example, secure hosting or a form/ spreadsheet tool), who may process data strictly on our instructions and only to the extent needed to provide their service to us;
  • Legal requirements, where disclosure is required by law, a court, or a lawful government request.

These are not sales or trades of your data, and such providers are not permitted to use your data for their own marketing.

4.Data retention

We keep your personal data only for as long as needed to provide our services and for legitimate business or legal purposes. When it is no longer required, we delete or anonymise it.

5.How we protect your data

We use reasonable security practices and procedures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your information appropriately.

6.Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or update inaccurate data;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • withdraw consent you have given, at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at team@jointryb.in and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7.Children

TRYB is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

8.Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always the one published on this page, with the "last updated" date above.

9.Contact & grievances

For any questions, requests, or grievances about your data or this policy, please contact:
