The evening

Some evenings need an excuse to start. A board game is a very good one.

Join TRYB at Agama Cafe for an evening that opens with games and ends somewhere far more interesting. We start with something easy to pick up — no small talk survives a close finish — and once the table has warmed up, the games stay out while dinner and conversation take over.

And, of course, you'll be doing it with a small group of people from TRYB. Come as you are, come a little competitive, and come ready to meet a few new people.

What's included

  • Board games for the evening, set up and ready to play
  • Your seat at a reserved TRYB table at Agama Cafe
  • A small TRYB group, curated with an equal male-to-female ratio
  • Time to play, eat, talk and stay a while

What's not included

  • Food and beverages — ordered at the table and paid for directly
  • Travel to and from the venue
  • Personal expenses
  • Anything not specifically mentioned under inclusions

A few things to know

  • The ₹299 covers the board games and your curated seat — your food and drinks are settled at the cafe on the night.
  • Please arrive a few minutes before 8:00 PM so the table can start together.
  • No experience with board games needed — everything we bring is easy to learn in a round.
  • The exact table details and final instructions will be shared with confirmed participants.
