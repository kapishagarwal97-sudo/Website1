03 — Method

The process

Five moves from a rough spark to something real and out in the world. Simple, deliberate, and always a little in your hands.

01

Spark

We start with the rawest version of your idea — no polish required. A conversation, a whiteboard, a voice note. We listen for the tension worth chasing and name what "good" would feel like.

Output: a one-page brief and a first hunch.

02

Shape

We explore directions fast and cheaply — sketches, references, moodboards, rough flows. The goal is to make the idea tangible enough to react to before anyone commits.

Output: concepts, a chosen direction, and a plan.

03

Build

Craft time. We design and build in tight loops, sharing work early and often so nothing drifts. You see it grow week by week, not all at once at the end.

Output: a working, considered first version.

04

Try

We put it in front of real people. We watch, we listen, we measure what matters — then we cut what doesn't and sharpen what does. Trying is the point, not the risk.

Output: honest feedback and a refined build.

05

Launch & grow

We ship it well, tell its story, and stay close as it finds its feet. Great things rarely end at launch — we help it keep getting better.

Output: a live product and a roadmap.
