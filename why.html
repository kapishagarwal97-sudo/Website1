01 — Story

Why tryb

A name is a promise. Ours is the smallest, bravest word there is — try — with room at the end for whatever you make of it.

The idea

Most things never begin. Not because they aren't good — but because beginning feels heavy. It asks you to be seen before you feel ready. TRYB exists to make that first move lighter.

We believe the rough draft is sacred. The half-idea, the demo that barely runs, the sketch on a napkin. That's where everything real starts, and it deserves a place that treats it with care instead of judgement.

Why the missing letter

You'll notice it — tryb, not "tribe". The word is deliberately unfinished. It's an invitation, not a full stop. The "b" is where you come in: build, begin, belong.

A tribe is who you gather. A try is what you dare. TRYB is both at once.

What we hold true
/ 01

Start small, start now
