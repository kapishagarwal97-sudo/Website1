Who are we

We're the reason five strangers become a story.

TRYB is a company built for real life. We're a small team of hosts, matchmakers and quiet romantics who believe the best part of any week is the evening you didn't see coming.

We noticed something simple: people have hundreds of followers and no one to call on a Tuesday. So we built the opposite of a feed — a place that gently puts you in a room with people worth meeting, and then gets out of the way.

No profiles to perfect. No swiping. Just an invitation, a table, and the beautiful awkward magic of saying hello.
