The evening

One table at Di Ghent Cafe, a small TRYB group, and an evening with nowhere else to be. Dinner starts at eight and runs as long as the conversation does — with cards on the table and a few suggested topics sent to you beforehand, so nothing has to start from a cold silence.

You'll be seated with a few people you haven't met, matched by TRYB. Before the evening you'll receive short profiles of everyone else at your table, and why we placed you together. Come as you are, order what you like, and leave with a story.

What's included

  • Your seat at a reserved TRYB table at Di Ghent Cafe
  • A small group, curated by TRYB
  • Profiles of everyone at your table before the dinner, and why you were matched
  • An evening to eat, talk and stay a while

What's not included

  • Food and beverages — ordered at the table and paid for directly
  • Travel, and anything not specifically mentioned under inclusions

A few things to know

  • ₹299 is a curation fee — your food and drinks are settled at the cafe on the night.
  • Please arrive a few minutes before 8:00 PM so the table can start together.
  • The exact table details and final instructions are shared with confirmed participants.
