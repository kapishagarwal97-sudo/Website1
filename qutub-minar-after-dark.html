The evening

You've probably seen Qutub Minar a hundred times. But have you ever experienced it after dark?

Join TRYB for an evening walk through one of Delhi's most iconic heritage sites, as the Qutub complex comes alive under the night sky. Discover stories hidden in its architecture, explore the history behind the monuments, and experience Qutub Minar in a completely different light.

And, of course, you'll be doing it with a small group of people from TRYB. Come curious, come comfortable, and come ready to meet a few new people along the way.

What's included

  • Curated guided night walk around the Qutub Minar complex
  • Historical storytelling and insights about the monuments
  • A chance to explore the complex after dark
  • A small TRYB group to experience it with
  • Time to walk, talk and discover

What's not included

  • Travel to and from the venue
  • Food and beverages
  • Personal expenses
  • Anything not specifically mentioned under inclusions

A few things to know

  • Comfortable footwear is recommended as this is a walking experience.
  • Please arrive a few minutes before the scheduled start time.
  • The experience takes place outdoors and may be subject to weather or venue related changes.
  • The exact meeting point and final instructions will be shared with confirmed participants.
