How it works

Four steps, from hello to a story.

step 1

Start with a short, human personality read so we can find the people you'll genuinely click with.

step 2

From candlelit dinners to rooftop sundowners, pottery to long morning walks. You pick — we'll plan every detail.

We match you with five to seven of your most compatible people. After dinner, groups from across the city come together for the night.

A quick follow-up after the evening. If the feeling's mutual, we'll pair you again — for another experience, or a blind date.
