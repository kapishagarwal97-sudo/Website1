The session

Discover the art of pottery in a two-hour, hands-on workshop. It opens with a live demonstration, and then the wheel is yours — with guidance first, and on your own after that.

This isn't a shape-some-clay-at-a-table workshop. You'll work on a real potter's wheel, three separate times — more turns than most workshops give you.

Built for complete beginners and for anyone after a genuinely therapeutic couple of hours. Every material you need is provided. And, as always, you'll be doing it with a small group from TRYB — so come curious, come ready to get your hands dirty, and come ready to meet a few new people.

What you'll do

  • The demonstration — a full walkthrough of crafting a pot on the potter's wheel, with the process and techniques explained as you watch.
  • Your first two pots — step-by-step guidance through the whole pot-making process.
  • Your third pot — the wheel is yours. Test what you've picked up and throw one on your own.

What's included

  • A guided pottery session on a real potter's wheel
  • A live demonstration before you start
  • Three turns at the wheel — two guided, one solo
  • All clay, tools and materials
  • Your pots to take home in their raw form
  • A small TRYB group, curated with an equal male-to-female ratio

What's not included

  • Firing and glazing of your pots — available at an extra cost after the workshop
  • Travel to and from the venue
  • Food and beverages
  • Anything not specifically mentioned under inclusions

At the end of the session

You're welcome to take your crafted pots home in their raw form — that's included. If you'd like them fired or glazed, that can be arranged after the workshop at an additional cost.

A few things to know

  • Please arrive before the start time. Slots are fixed, so if you're late that time comes out of your session — it can't be extended at the end.
  • Wear something you don't mind getting clay on.
  • All materials are provided, so there's nothing you need to bring.
  • The exact studio address and final instructions will be shared with confirmed participants.
